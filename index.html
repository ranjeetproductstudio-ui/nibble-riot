Privacy Policy

Offline game · No ads · No analytics

About this policy

Nibble Riot is published by Ranjeet Product Studio (GitHub account: ranjeetproductstudio-ui). This policy applies to the offline Android game, version 0.1.2, package com.nibble.riot.android, and earlier offline versions 0.1.0 and 0.1.1 released under package com.nibbleriot.game. Effective date: 25 October 2026.

Information the game uses

The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal or sensitive user data. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, advertising ID or other device identifiers. It has no accounts, online multiplayer, cloud saves or server connection, and requests no Android permissions.

Settings stored on your device

The game stores only your music and sound-effects preferences in its private app storage so that it can remember your audio choices. Gameplay state is held in memory. These settings are not sent to the developer or third parties. Android app backup is disabled for this build.

Advertising, analytics and purchases

These versions contain no advertisements, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, third-party crash-reporting SDKs or in-app purchases. If a future version introduces services that handle data, this policy and the Google Play disclosures will be updated before those practices begin.

Security, retention and deletion

Local preferences use Android’s app-private storage. The developer operates no server storing gameplay or personal data from these versions. You can delete the local preferences by clearing the app’s storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app. There is no game account or server-side game profile to delete.

Children’s privacy

The same no-collection practices apply to all players, including children. The game has no chat, registration or features that ask players to provide personal information.

Support and privacy inquiries

Contact ranjeetproductstudio@gmail.com for questions about Nibble Riot or this policy. If you choose to email us, your address and the information you send are handled by the email service and used to respond to your inquiry. Include only information necessary for your request. Support correspondence is retained only as needed to address the request or meet applicable legal obligations; you may request its deletion at the same address.

This policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Visiting it is separate from playing the offline game. GitHub may process technical information about website requests under its own privacy statement. This page contains no analytics scripts, advertising, tracking pixels, cookies set by us or forms.

Changes

The effective date above identifies this policy revision. Changes to the game’s data practices will be reflected on this page. You can contact us using the email above about any revision.
